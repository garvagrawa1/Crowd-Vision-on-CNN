How CrowdVision Works

Every camera frame goes through the same six steps, in about 50–150 ms on a laptop CPU.

01 / PIPELINE
01

Capture

Grab a webcam or video frame and resize it to 640 px.

02

Image Pyramid

Smaller copies (×0.75 each) so every face becomes ~48 px in one copy.

03

CrowdVisionNet

Our own fully convolutional CNN scores every 48×48 square in one pass.

04

Clustered NMS

Merge overlapping boxes with voting + nested-box rule: one box per face.

05

Tracker

Each face keeps an ID across frames; the count is the median of 15 frames.

06

RecogNet

Our second CNN names enrolled people or marks the face Unknown.

About the Project

CrowdVision: Custom CNN-Based Face Detection, Crowd Counting and Face Recognition.

06 / BRIEF

Problem

Manual crowd monitoring is slow and error-prone. Staff cannot watch every area at once, so unsafe crowd density is noticed late.

Our approach

  • CrowdVisionNet: own fully convolutional CNN (122,774 parameters), trained from scratch on ~166,000 crops
  • Hard-negative mining on 40,000 of its own mistakes
  • Clustered NMS + nested-box rule + tracker: one steady box per face
  • RecogNet: second own CNN for recognising enrolled people

Results (unseen test data)

  • 99.87% crop-level accuracy
  • 96.1% precision · 99.0% recall · F1 97.5% on 300 crowd scenes
  • Counting error: 0.23 people per image

Where it's used

Schools and colleges, airports, railway stations, shopping malls, hospitals and public events.

Future enhancements

  • Full-body detection for people facing away
  • Density-map branch for very dense crowds
  • Low-light and side-profile faces
  • Edge devices and multi-camera dashboard
